PADAWAN FAQ

Do I need my own custom lightsaber to join?

No! MUFS provides loaner LED stunt lightsabers for practice sessions. Everyone is welcome regardless of gear.

Are there club dues or fees?

General membership and weekly meetings are completely free for registered Miami University students!

Can non-Miami students or alumni participate?

Our primary membership is for current undergraduate & graduate students, but community events and select open dueling sessions are open to alumni and guests! Especially during large Geek Con events, all are welcome!
